Quiet foundations. A little sunshine.

White and warm neutrals hold the page together. Yellow signals a selected prompt or a user’s question. Pastels live mainly in the artwork and feature surfaces.

  • Primary buttons use #171a17 and white text. The hero retains its existing #161816 ink and #60645f description.
  • Keep existing local colour variations. This palette is a reference, not a reason to recolour finished components.
  • Use readable ink for text. Pastel text, neon accents and a new dark theme do not belong to this website.

Quick feedback. Readable stories.

Motion should explain what the AI is doing. Reuse the shared clock and existing sequences; preserve finished states when the visitor scrolls back.

  • Desktop chat travels between hero and examples without resetting. On phones it does not travel.
  • Desktop backend windows open on hover/focus. On phones, tool details expand inside the chat and can be reopened by touch.
  • Keep reduced-motion content fully readable. Pause offscreen videos and renderers. Never tie video playback speed or a popup’s interaction timing to scroll velocity.

Talk like a helpful trader friend.

Lead with the action someone can take. Keep the language concrete and approachable. Leave Python, tokens and implementation details for the places where they help explain a result.

Read charts, draw levels and keep watch, all from your chat.
  • Use short, direct sentences. Avoid em dashes, jargon, exaggerated promises and generic AI marketing language.
  • Indian markets are supported now. International markets are coming soon. Do not invent new integrations or an account-creation URL.
  • The AI builds the trading tool; /markets supplies the market data and calculations. It is not a separate app builder, financial adviser or autonomous order-execution product.
  • Accuracy: 7/8 versus 4/8 in one eight-question test. 90% is rounded from 87.5%, not an overall accuracy rate.
  • Cost: 86% is an assumption-based API-cost estimate, not a measured universal saving or a cheaper fixed-price chat subscription.
  • Keep the details popups and disclaimer. Do not invent trader names, attributed testimonials, test runs or performance figures.

Keep the small details intact.

  1. Read the guide and the component’s current code. Make the smallest complete change.
  2. Check 1440px desktop, 390px and 320px mobile. No page overflow, edge-stuck cards, clipped copy or changing chat heights.
  3. Test keyboard focus, mobile navigation, popup close/outside/Escape, replay and reduced motion for the component you changed.
  4. For animation work, test both scroll directions and refresh in a later section. Preserve the conversation’s progress.
  5. Check syntax, relevant tests, missing assets and browser errors. Keep font, icon and shader license notices.
  6. Keep claims, their explanations and the disclaimer together. Publish or push only when the user asks.
