How agents use markets.

Your agent writes the script. markets runs it and draws the chart.

script.pysent by your agent
# "How has NVIDIA traded around $185?"
from markets import bars, chart

nvda = bars("NVDA", "1h", days=8)
level = 185

chart.show(nvda)
chart.hline(level, label="$185")
chart.mark(nvda.touches(level))

Important information

/markets does not train AI models on market data. AI agents are only an input channel: they send Python scripts, which /markets checks and runs in a sandbox on its own servers. Scripts can read data and draw on charts. They cannot place orders, reach the internet or export data.

Market data is delayed by at least 15 minutes. Charts and prices are shown only on /markets; your agent receives a link to the chart, not prices.

/markets is a charting and analytics application, not a financial adviser. It does not give investment advice or recommendations, show returns, place trades or offer virtual trading, contests or games. /markets is not registered with SEBI as an investment adviser or research analyst.
