Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

/markets is operated by Certus Innovations Pvt Ltd (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how we handle personal data collected on this website, in line with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist, we collect your email address. We do not collect any other personal data on this website.

Why we use it

We use your email only to tell you about /markets, including when your access is ready. We do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep your email until you ask us to delete it, or until we no longer need it for the purpose above.

Your rights

You can withdraw your consent, ask to see, correct or delete your data, or raise a grievance at any time by writing to privacy@slash.markets. Every email we send also lets you unsubscribe.

Market data

/markets does not train AI models on market data and does not share market data with anyone to train theirs.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
