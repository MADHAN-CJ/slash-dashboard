slash.markets is a charting and analytics application. Everything on this website and in the product is for research and education.

## Not investment advice

slash.markets is not registered with SEBI as an investment adviser or research analyst, and it is not a financial adviser. It does not give investment advice, tell you what to buy or sell, or forecast prices. It does not show returns, place trades, or offer virtual trading, contests or games.

Trading involves risk, including loss of capital. Your trading decisions are your own.

## Orders and access

slash.markets places no orders and has read-only access. Python code that your AI sends to slash.markets runs in a sandbox on its own servers. The code can read market data and draw on charts. It cannot place orders or reach the internet.

slash.markets does not train AI models on market data.

## Market data and alerts

Market data in slash.markets is delayed by at least <Fact name="data.delayMinutes" /> minutes. Data, calculations and alerts may contain errors, and alerts can be delayed.

## Answers from your AI

Your AI app writes its own answers from the data and results slash.markets returns. AI apps can make mistakes, so check important numbers yourself before you rely on them.

## Examples on this website

Prompts, outputs, charts, prices and notifications shown on this website are illustrative. They use sample or past data, with the date stated. They never show a real user's account, trades or results.

## Comparisons and lists

We make slash.markets. When an article compares tools or ranks products, it says so. We rank products, never securities, and every fact about another product links its official source with the date we checked it.
