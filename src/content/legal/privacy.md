/markets is operated by Certus Innovations Pvt Ltd (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how we handle personal data collected on this website, in line with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

## What we collect

When you join the waitlist, we collect your email address. With it, we store the time you joined, the page you joined from, the page that linked you there (the referrer) and your browser details (the user agent, which names your browser and operating system).

We also use Google Analytics on this website, as described below.

## Analytics

We use Google Analytics to see how people use this website. It records the pages you visit, the site that sent you here, whether you joined the waitlist, your device and browser, and your approximate location, which Google works out from your IP address. It sets cookies so it can recognise a return visit. Google explains [how it uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). You can block Google Analytics in your browser settings or with Google’s [opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Why we use it

We use your email only to tell you about /markets, including when your access is ready. We use the page, referrer and browser details stored with it, and Google Analytics, to understand how people find and use this website. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

## How long we keep it

We keep your email and the details stored with it until you ask us to delete them, or until we no longer need them for the purposes above.

## Your rights

You can withdraw your consent, ask to see, correct or delete your data, or raise a grievance at any time by writing to [privacy@slash.markets](mailto:privacy@slash.markets). Every email we send also lets you unsubscribe.

## Market data

/markets does not train AI models on market data and does not share market data with anyone to train theirs.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
